# False Objectivity in F. Scott Fitzgerald's The Great Gatsby

While the [[diction]] and [[characterization]] frame [[Nick Carraway|Nick]] as a detached narrator, Nick is also characterized as someone who hides details and an [[unreliable narrator]], conveying a theme that those who attempt to preserve true neutrality amid the subjective entanglements of society will fail or lie in saying that they succeeded.

The diction and direct characterization frame Nick as a detached narrator. 

	

Work Cited
Fitzgerald, F. Scott. [[The Great Gatsby]]. 1925. New York City, Scribner, 2004.
