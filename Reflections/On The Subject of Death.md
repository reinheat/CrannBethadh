Enough preamble, without further ado, on the subject of death. 

Life has a certain quality to it, I remember hearing recently in a recorded lecture that it is special because not all rocks have life to them; it is special because the "rocks" we are recognize that we have sapience and that we are alive, barring any talks of if that is true for a different writing session. 

When we consider the "value" of a life, and for the sake of discussion we will place a value on a life in Life Value with 10 being a perfect life and 1 being the worst life still worth living, we must consider that in nearly all cases the LV will be above 0 and as such be something worth living. However, in spite of that we can do a sort of calculation of Life Value based on two factors, I would chart this if able; on the x-axis we can put the length of a life, it is fair to say that a longer life will be more fulfilling and have more value than a shorter life; on the y-axis we can put the quality of living, perhaps this measured on some unit of time such as years or months. Through this I will present a quandary, say we have a life that is lived to its fullest length but perhaps not to a high quality, a 10 length but a 1 quality; by our metrics this life is a 10 Value but I would be hard pressed to find anyone who would willing want to be put in this life. Why? We can consider that there are many factors to that, but I believe most present is the idea that "living" and "living" are not the same; that is to say that the countenance of life is not living in the sense of fulfillment. To present the counter quandary, we have a life that is lived to its fullest quality but not to its fullest length, note here that this is not a middle length but rather a notably short length, in other words a 1 length but a 10 quality. Again, this is a 10 Value, but again I would be hard pressed to find anyone willing to take this life.
