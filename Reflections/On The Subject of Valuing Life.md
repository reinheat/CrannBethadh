Enough preamble, without further ado, on the subject of valuing life. 

Life has a certain quality to it, I remember hearing recently in a recorded lecture that it is special because not all rocks have life to them; it is special because the "rocks" we are recognize that we have sapience and that we are alive, barring any talks of if that is true for a different writing session. 

When we consider the "value" of a life, and for the sake of discussion we will place a value on a life in Life Value with 10 being a perfect life and 1 being the worst life still worth living, we must consider that in nearly all cases the LV will be above 0 and as such be something worth living. However, in spite of that we can do a sort of calculation of Life Value based on two factors, I would chart this if able; on the x-axis we can put the Length of a Life, it is fair to say that a longer life will be more fulfilling and have more value than a shorter life; on the y-axis we can put the Quality of Living, perhaps this measured on some unit of time such as years or months. Through this I will present a quandary, say we have a life that is lived to its fullest length but perhaps not to a high quality, a 10 length but a 1 quality; by our metrics this life is a 10 Value but I would be hard pressed to find anyone who would willing want to be put in this life. Why? We can consider that there are many factors to that, but I believe most present is the idea that "living" and "living" are not the same; that is to say that the countenance of life is not living in the sense of fulfillment. To present the counter quandary, we have a life that is lived to its fullest quality but not to its fullest length, note here that this is not a middle length but rather a notably short length, in other words a 1 length but a 10 quality. Again, this is a 10 Value, but again I would be hard pressed to find anyone willing to take this life. Why? If we consider that living and living are not the same then this is your perfect life, one where the sense of fulfillment is had to its fullest. Now you may be having thoughts on that argument, it may seem silly but I would remind you that that is the point, obviously the trust answer lies somewhere in the middle ground. 

Enough on quality, lets talk purpose. Now purpose will in some way contribute to the Quality of Living, that much I believe we can say is obvious but we as thinking rocks in some aspect can recognize our limited time; we are perhaps uniquely able to realize that our time is  not infinite and that one day we will die. This is the concern of most philosophy, the idea of futility in face of death. Two schools of thought come to mind, those being [[Nihilism]], the idea that it is pointless to worry as it is doomed to happen; as well as [[Absurdism]], the idea that the universe is indifferent to you and that things are naturally Absurd.
