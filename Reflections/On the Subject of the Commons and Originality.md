Before I begin, I'll point out the inherent poshness of the title. By Commons I mean the [[Creative Commons]] not any kind of judgement on the class of creativity in any work of media. 

# On the Subject of The Commons and Originality. 

I recently was rediscovering a tool for sifting through public domain films and using them for your own works. It sparked me to think on the nature of creativity when placed in a world that is derivative by nature. In fact, this is a problem that I've struggled with myself on many occasions; not feeling my work was original, thinking that it was a copy because my creativity was derivative from another work. I am forced to confront that feeling when working in the domain of the Commons.

So, I believe we must ask: if the work is derivative and wholly unoriginal in its parts, then is it an origin work? Or does it not holistically matter? Is the sum or the parts equating to its creativity integrity. 

Take a work like [[Star Wars]], I think we would all say, even if we would argue the quality, that it is a creative work. It's a safe assessment though it is openly a fusion of [[Kurosawa]], [[Dune]], and WW2 era dog fighting. Now, of course, there is the obvious argument that the Force was in none of those, but if I wrote my own Star Wars and it was just Dune (simplifying for the argument) but on that sandy planet they had a mystical connection to the world, would it be as creative? If I copy just one thing is it a creative work or derivative?
