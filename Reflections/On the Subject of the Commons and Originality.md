Before I begin, I'll point out the inherent poshness of the title. By Commons I mean the [[Creative Commons]] not any kind of judgement on the class of creativity in any work of media. 

# On the Subject of The Commons and Originality. 

I recently was rediscovering a tool for sifting through public domain films and using them for your own works. It sparked me to think on the nature of creativity when placed in a world that is derivative by nature. In fact, this is a problem that I've struggled with myself on many occasions; not feeling my work was original, thinking that it was a copy because my creativity was derivate from another work. I am forced to confront that feeling when working in the domain of the Commons.

So, I believe we must ask"  if the work is derivative and wholly unoriginal in its parts, then is it an origin work? Or does it not wholistically matter? Is the sum or the parts equating to its creativity integrity.
