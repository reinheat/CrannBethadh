NOthing that meets whatever standard of creative integrity that I foolishly hold. It is undoubtly foolish, I have no critics, hell I have no readers. The words I pen go to an audience of nobody for critics with no eyes nor mouths to express judgment, and yet I weigh myself in the eyes of those who do not exist. Like rain in a desert, it is a hope that I cannot have but for which I will always desire. Poigant as that may or may not be, it does not solve my issue, I lack ideas.
