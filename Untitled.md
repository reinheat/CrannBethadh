I've always struggled to write reflective essays, at least any that would have substance. I've always found myself just meandering over word choices or other minute details like that. And truly, those details do not matter. In fact, they hamper whatever writing goal I tend to have. Be it a creative matter like writing a story or a more observational piece like this. Truly, that detail of myself is a flaw, though perhaps a flaw that I could keep if pressed. In another topic, I've yet to title this piece; in fact, I'm not even sure what I intend to write in this. I was just certain that putting words on a page would be in some way beneficial to myself. In what way or manner remains to be seen, it is one of the benefits to being a "by-the-seat-of-your-pants" writer. Though, I was never cut out for long form writing, novels is what I mean here. I struggled with character dialogue and how to make it sound natural. It's a tricky bastard that. Your dialogue must be both well written and progressive to the plot; neither of which are something that comes iniately to me. I wrote about this subject once in a private page, but I doubt that this page will gain enough traction from anyone to be suitably revealing to my personal life so I'll share regardless a certain truth about myself. My body is weak. If you knew me in person then surely you'd think the same thing. I am thin and gaunt with pisspoor stamina and a bad sleep cycle. I once theorized that it was that nature that led me to living through fiction. To play dnd and put stake of my identity in it. I believe verbaitem I said something akin to my body cannot do heroic acts but my mind and words can orate them. If not that then something similar, at least with the same sentiment. There is a very, and I mean very, slim chance that one of my close friends could see and read this message. I sincerly doubt it, I only provided the Crann Bethadh, in its incomplete beauty, to share mu [[Creative Ideas MOC]], but hypothetically they could chance upon this page whilst sifting my catalogues. I suppose if they take that fine comb then they deserve to find whatever they chance upon. 

It would seem that I've returned to the ultimate issue of this note, that is of course what to write about. An issue, that plagues me near ad infitum. Everyday it feels like I should be writing something and yet nbothing. No matter what my ambition tells me, I cannot write or come up with an idea worth anything or that holds any amount of merit. NOthing that meets whatever standard of creative integrity that I foolishly hold. It is undoubtly foolish, I have no critics, hell I have no readers. The words I pen go to an audience of nobody for critics with no eyes nor mouths to express judgment, and yet I weigh myself in the eyes of those who do not exist. Like rain in a desert, it is a hope that I cannot have but for which I will always desire. Poigant as that may or may not be, it does not solve my issue, I lack ideas. I am a drought mind, absent of thought and full of only desire for great aspirations.
