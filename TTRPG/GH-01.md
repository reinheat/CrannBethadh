Squad 3 is dispatched to Gaspé Bay, Quebec. The Sin type is a CAT 1, Type 1 (SEVERED), OGRE sin. The Sin host is [[Thomas Riel]]. 

>[!info] Sin Type OGRE
>Sins born of inadequacy, failure, shame, and self-hatred. 
>
>DOMAINS:
>1. Unseeing of Things
>2. Where You Belong
>3. The Lash Calls You Brother

The Host, Thomas Riel is a young deckhand. Last winter, on Nightwatch, a boat got in trouble on the rocks and RIEL froze and watched it sink. He holds a great surviors guilt over the event, self isolating in a sort of self-punishment that led to the formation of an OGRE class sin. 

**WHO OR WHAT PUSHED YOU INTO THIS WHOLE?**
	The crash that night. I keep thinking about how my cowardly nature was the difference between life and death for those people.
**WHO OR WHAT IS KEEPING YOU FROM GOING OVER THE EDGE?**
	The crew (my coworkers at the dock). They still believe in me, they don't know what I failed to do that night. The comfort of their words is what keeps me sane.
**WHAT ARE YOU ASHAMED OF?**
	That my fear will spread. The only reason bad people like me survive when good people like them die is so that we can spread our fear. 
# MIASMA
Each OGRE brings with it a MIASMA, this one has a heavy fog with heavy snow inside the fog. The fog eminates from the shipwreck and covers the affected area with a thick layer of snow.

# PALACE
The interior of RIEL's trauma is the weathered down shipwreck. The OGRE itself is resting inside the shipwreck, partially submerged peeking out at night to bring terror and trauma reminders to RIEL during his nightwatches. 

## THE NAIL OF ABEL
The Nail of Abel for RIEL is a baggie he keeps in his dorm. The baggie contains a family photograph recovered from the shipwreck. For RIEL, this is a symbol of his failure and a way to remind himself of how his hesitant nature brought ruin to someone's life.
