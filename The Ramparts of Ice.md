I have just recently finished The Ramparts of Ice, a romance manga regarding the troubled life of introvert Koyuki Hikawa and her budding romance for Minato Amamiya. The love story of these two is in tandem with the love story of Yota Hino and Miki Azumi, both friends of Koyuki and Minato. 

The story of these four high schoolers is one of self conscious introspection and doubt. In nearly every problem there exists some element of it in which one of the characters is making an assumption of another. For instance, in the beginning our main character, Koyuki is portrayed as an "Ice Queen," someone who is overly reclusive; whilst Minato is portrayed as somewhat of a locksmith, someone who can get people to open up to him. Minato gets an interest in Koyuki and as such attempts to "pick" her and in doing so Koyuki assumes he is picking on her and as such tells him off.
