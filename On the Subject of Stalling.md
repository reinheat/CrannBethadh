This is a more personal reflection. I am between things, that is to say that I am out of a job, meant to be growing up, and waiting for school to start. By all metrics I am inert and unmoving like a block of iron. To me, there is no meaning in time. My 4pms feel the same as my 2ams feel the same as midnights and middays. If the goal of my maturing is to become a civilized person, then by all means I have reverted to a primal state. Like a bear hibernating in winter. 

I suppose then that is why I have sought companionship, through a sort of, not parasitic but, symbiotic(?) relationship I can quasi experience a life through them. It is a selfish desire, to experience that. To desire them for that goal. However, does it matter? Do I need a reason for seeking them? Does any reason matter over the action? I doubt so, I doubt that it is the case. 

Liminal spaces are defined, at least to me, as a space that is normally percieved in one way being percieved without it. A space without, is an eloquent way to put it. For instance, a classroom with no people, or perhaps an airport terminal lacking any travelers. These spaces feel weird, almost unnatural because they are lacking. That is an interesting term though. To be lacking carries a negative connotation. If it is lacking then it most have a state of unlacking that we'd expect it to be in. We expect an airport terminal to have people, we expect a classroom to have students. 

The phase I am in is also called liminality. In times of past, there were rituals and gods for these period. Janus, the Greek god of portals, comes to mind. So to do birthday parties and graduations. But for my state to be liminality means that I am in a liminal time. That I am lacking and am *meant* to be unlacking. That I will be filled, that I will be fixed I suppose.
