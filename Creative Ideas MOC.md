 This will serve as a location to put my creative works, note that ideas here are likely unfinished and rough in terms of quality, they will be sorted in no particular order.

- [[Glies]] - This setting is heavily inspired by island cultures, it features yokai and other folklore type monsters. The main conflict of Glies  is a mist rolling in from a land and the disrupted winds that serve as the main routes for transportation.
- [[Yellow Dancer]] - This setting is heavily inspired by [[Cyberpunk]] and the [[Project Moon]] franchise. Inside the dystopian city, not all is right after the invention and ruinous collapse of magic leaves the city in a state of disarray; the wealth inequalities resulting in corporate controlled safe havens and no central power to protect the people. 
- [[NIGHTHAWKS]] - A work in progress web browser mystery game. Explore the Academy and investigate the death of Cecily Smith and how she mysteriously died in a room with no murderers. 
- [[No Light in Zalinski]] - An urban war horror game, inspired by the [[S.T.A.L.K.E.R]] franchise. In the war torn city of Zalinksi all communications are down and the last rescue vessel is on course to enter a minefield. Will you be able to get to the radio tower and warn them before their demise?
- [[Death's Due]] - A pirate game, inspired by [[Pirates of the Caribbean]], steal the vessel of death and see if you can manage to pawn it off before Death returns the favor.
